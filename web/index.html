Read the history in context

“Deficient”, “Non critical” and “Complied / Corrected during inspection” are the province’s terms at the time of each inspection. A remedy describes the required action. Non-critical findings may remain displayed until the next scheduled inspection. The province says reports are normally posted within three days; its upload timestamp appears with each search.
